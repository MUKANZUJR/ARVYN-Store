🚚 Delivery & Collection Policy

We offer flexible delivery and pick-up options to make shopping simple and convenient.

Weekdays (Mon–Fri)

  • Pick-Up Hours: 1:00 PM – 1:50 PM & 5:00 PM – 6:30 PM
  • Delivery Hours: 9:00 AM – 4:30 PM
  • Customers may send their own rider or request delivery through us (fees apply).

Weekends (Sat–Sun)

  • Pick-Up & Delivery: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
Important Notes:
  • Pick-up details are shared privately after confirmation.
  • Please collect or arrange delivery within 48 hours once notified your order is ready.
  • Delivery fees are non-refundable once a rider has been dispatched.

💸 Returns & Refund Policy

We want you to love every purchase! If there’s an issue with your order, please review our simple return and refund terms below 👇

Returns

  • Returns are accepted only for defective, damaged, or incorrect items.
  • Notify us within 24 hours of receiving your order.
  • Items must be unused, in original packaging, and in the same condition received.
  • Proof (photo or short video) may be requested before approval.

Refunds

  • Approved refunds are processed within 1–3 working days via your original payment method.
  • Delivery fees are non-refundable.

Exchanges

  • Exchanges allowed for the same item type if available.
  • If out of stock, you may choose a different item of equal value or request a refund.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Wigs, hair extensions, and beauty accessories once opened
  • Intimate wear
  • Sale or clearance items

💬 For assistance with a return or exchange, please contact us through our official business WhatsApp or inbox.
Your satisfaction matters to us!
